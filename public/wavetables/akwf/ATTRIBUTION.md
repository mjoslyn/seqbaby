The single-cycle `.wav` waveforms in this directory are from **AKWF — Adventure
Kid Waveforms**, a collection of single-cycle waveforms created by **Kristoffer
Karl Axel Ekstrand** (Adventure Kid).

- Repository: https://github.com/KristofferKarlAxelEkstrand/AKWF-FREE
- Author: Kristoffer Karl Axel Ekstrand — https://www.adventurekid.se

Only a small subset of the full AKWF collection is bundled here (see
`WAVETABLE_WAVES` in `public/js/voices.js`). The waveforms are used as-is as
source material for the wavetable engine (`wt:akwf`) and the in-app wavetable
editor.
